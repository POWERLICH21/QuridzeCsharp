How to play & payouts

Duel rules: both players bet once, before round 1. Each round both armies draw five cards from the crypt, dealt in pairs of similar value. Each card shows the points it gives, and its colour shows its level; matching cards join into one pile (x2, x3). Your opponent's cards stay face down until the end of each round. Each round you secretly plan one spell: Animate Dead doubles one of your cards and upgrades it, Implosion destroys one of theirs. Press Ready; when both players are ready, both spells take effect together (a card that is doubled and destroyed in the same round is destroyed) and both armies are revealed.

After three rounds the stronger army wins: the winner gets their bet back plus winnings of bet × 0.20 × (their points ÷ 100), and the Crypt Fortune may multiply those winnings (Lucky ×2, BIG ×10, MEGA ×50). The loser's bet goes to the Necropolis; a draw returns both bets.
