Rules
  • Against Claude you play White. Claude only gets Black's view: the squares Black can see, Black's moves, and what Black learned from yours.
  • Against a friend, the board is covered between turns. Pass the screen and let the other player reveal it.
  • You see the squares next to your pieces, one step in every direction. Everything else is fog.
  • There is no check or checkmate. Capturing the enemy king wins, so guard yours.
  • You may move into the fog. A rook, bishop or queen that runs into a hidden enemy stops there and captures it.
  • A pawn's two-square first move stops after one square if a hidden piece is in the way.
  • Castling fails, and costs you the turn, if a hidden piece stands between king and rook.
  • En passant and promotion work as usual.
  • Notation is long algebraic. ▒▒ marks a square you could not see.
